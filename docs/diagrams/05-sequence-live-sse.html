Live request & SSE subscription

The SPA crosses the nginx auth_basic boundary to the loopback API, then subscribes to /api/events — which tails the pipeline's events.jsonl until the client cap returns a 503.

Two auth layers

  • nginx auth_basic guards every /api/ proxy
  • Optional WEBCAM_API_TOKEN (Bearer/Basic) on mutating endpoints

SSE stream

  • /api/events tails the pipeline's events.jsonl by byte offset; catalog mtimes are the fallback
  • Emits image.new, new-detection, detection.preliminary, new-burst, plus a 15 s ping and an idle close

Bounded fan-out

  • WEBCAM_SSE_MAX_CLIENTS cap (default 8)
  • Heartbeat + idle timeout keep streams from leaking
