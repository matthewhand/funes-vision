Timeline · funes-vision

One visit, from frame to deletion

Frames arrive over a short motion window, the fast YOLO pass marks each in under a second, and the deep model finishes about 40 s later for the hit frame. Contiguous presence is grouped into a visit, the gallery sees it live over SSE, and retention removes it after 30 days unless pinned.

A visit is a contiguous detector run

  • Frames within burst_threshold_seconds (300 s) of each other group into one burst keyed by the last frame
  • bursts.json is written with the group; burst_valid drops spans longer than the chain rule allows

Fast marks, deep explains

  • YOLO writes fast_pass=partial in well under a second, so detection.preliminary reaches the gallery first
  • The deep pass merges HA flags ~40 s later; detector labels are never overwritten

Live now, pruned later

  • SSE polls analysis.json / bursts.json mtime every ~3 s on the fallback path
  • Retention deletes analyzed-empty at 30 days or 5 GB; pins and LLM visits within the persist cap survive
