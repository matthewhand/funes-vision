Swimlane · funes-vision

A porch visit, lane by lane

A motion still is dropped over FTP, queued, gated by a fast YOLO pass, enriched by a local Ollama deep pass that attaches Home Assistant flags, merged into analysis.json and grouped into a visit in bursts.json, then surfaced live over SSE. Slack and HA MQTT are optional egress; pin-aware retention deletes the visit unless it is pinned.

Focal handoff

  • Deep Ollama run attaches the HA flags (postal_delivery, porch_access)
  • Only real YOLO hits reach the LLM — car-only frames are skipped
  • Served by local Ollama at localhost:11434

Pipeline & store

  • Fast YOLOv4-tiny pass on every frame
  • Merge keeps detector flags, attaches LLM flags
  • Visits grouped by time into bursts.json

Optional egress

  • SSE pushes detections to the SPA live
  • Slack notify is opted in
  • HA Compare topics are MQTT-retained
