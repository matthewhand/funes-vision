Failure, retry & recovery

The watchdog polls /api/health, then either restarts a stuck unit and forces a retention-only pass, or leaves a healthy pipeline alone. A second, independent 60 s timer guards the gallery container instead.

Watchdog is the safety net

  • Cron */15 check detects dead units and stale sweeps
  • Hourly retention forces --retention-only

Catalog self-heals

  • Truncated writes are repaired on load, not fatal
  • Retention still runs so ENOSPC cannot deadlock cleanup

Independent health probe

  • webcam-healthcheck.timer fires every 60s
  • It probes the gallery container — docker ps then curl :8180 — and does not call /api/health
