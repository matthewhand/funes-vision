Detection & image lifecycle

A camera's motion JPEG starts unanalyzed; the fast YOLO pass writes a preliminary record, and only a deep-pass hit becomes a merged LLM detection. Detector-only frames never spend the deep budget, and pinning is an orthogonal lifecycle.

Merged is the only verified kind

  • A deep pass that returns HA flags writes an _llm dict; detector labels are never overwritten (merge_llm_into_fastpass)
  • catalog.kind() reports verified only when _llm is non-empty and no _llm_skip is present

Parked cars skip the deep budget

  • A car-only hit is persisted as _llm_skip=no_trigger (detector-only) and never re-queued as urgent
  • Off by default, idle backfill (DEEP_BACKFILL=false) can later promote detector-only to merged

Retention order and pins

  • Pass 1 age (>30 d), Pass 2 persist cap (20% of 5 GB), Pass 3 disk (>5 GB): negatives, YOLO-only, persistable, then unanalyzed
  • Pins are never auto-deleted; LLM-verified visits may outlive the age cutoff within the persist cap
